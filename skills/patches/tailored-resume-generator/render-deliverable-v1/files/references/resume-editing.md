# Editing a resume for submission

Apply this editorial process before generating the PDF and Word files. The goal is a concise, differentiated account of real work, not a longer inventory of tools or a polished-looking audit report.

## 1. Establish positioning

Read the target role's seniority, core responsibilities, essential skills, and distinguishing requirements. Choose the strongest evidence-backed thread connecting the candidate to that role. A graduate may lead with education and relevant projects; an experienced candidate may lead with recent work. Do not assign a seniority, specialization, or ownership level the material does not establish.

Use the job description to decide emphasis and terminology. Do not transfer an employer's requirements into the candidate's skill list without evidence.

## 2. Build a fact and boundary checklist

Before writing, identify the following in the source material:

- Identity and context: employer, role, education, degree, dates, project name, team context, and any stated course, personal, open-source, experiment, or production setting. Treat learning channels such as self-study or formal training as facts requiring explicit support.
- Contribution: what the candidate did, what they coordinated or participated in, and what belonged to teammates.
- Methods and scope: actually used tools, technical steps, tested areas, supported scale, and stated proficiency.
- Results: supplied quantities, before/after measurements, measurement names, attribution, and environment.

Every added label and strengthened verb needs support. "Independent", "led", "architected", "proficient", "course project", "production deployment", "open-source", "self-taught", and "trained" are not harmless stylistic additions. Do not infer them from a project title, a technical stack, the fact that a person studied a tool, or the absence of another named contributor. Thin material warrants a shorter resume, not invented learning channels or claims of a solid foundation.

Preserve negation and constraints in the fact checklist. In the final resume, express the positive responsibility wherever that conveys the same boundary. Retain a qualifier when removing it changes the claim: team-level results, experimental measurements, and retrieval metrics still need their real meaning.

## 3. Select a main thread and distribute space

Rank evidence by relevance to the target, difficulty, candidate action, and verifiable outcome. Keep the strongest bullets first within each role: the technical contribution or known result that best proves the job requirements goes before routine coordination and supporting work. Do not preserve the source's process order merely because reviews, integration, implementation, and reports were described in that order. Use a few well-supported bullets to make each experience legible at a glance.

- Usually use 2–4 substantive bullets for one work or project experience; use fewer for genuinely thin material and more only when distinct evidence warrants it. This is a count of bullets per experience, not sentences per bullet: give each bullet one main contribution in one sentence, at most two.
- Preserve all relevant supplied roles and projects. Reduce repeated process narration inside an experience instead of deleting an entire relevant experience to meet a page target.
- Put tools next to the work they enabled. A compact categorized skills section can establish breadth, but should not repeat every technical detail from the experience section.
- Keep item titles and subtitles complementary. If a skills item is titled with its category, do not repeat that category as its subtitle. Omit a subtitle when it adds no information. Do not use repeated aliases or a long heading to fill space.
- Do not force a numerical result into every bullet. A concrete, source-backed scope or technical validation can be useful when no business metric exists.

## 4. Write achievement bullets

Prefer a concrete action plus a method or scope and, when supplied, a verifiable result. Not every bullet needs all components; each should carry distinct evidence.

Prioritize what the candidate solved, how they approached it, and what can be demonstrated. Combine related routine steps into one useful statement. Avoid repeated openings about attending meetings, following processes, preparing records, or "establishing mechanisms" without naming the actual work.

Use direct verbs that match the contribution: analyzed, implemented, tested, reproduced, verified, compared, maintained, or coordinated. Use leadership and ownership verbs only when supported. Do not replace concrete work with vague claims about empowerment, full-chain platforms, methodological systems, or business value.

Small transferable examples:

| Source | Appropriate editing | Unsupported editing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studied tool A; no practice was described | Studied tool A | Proficient in tool A and able to deliver independently |
| Participated in release testing; reproduced issues, collected evidence, and verified developers' fixes | Participated in release testing, reproduced defects, collected diagnostic evidence, and verified fixes | Independently led end-to-end system optimization |
| Measured a retrieval metric on a fixed experimental dataset after a teammate's configuration change | Compared the retrieval metric before and after the change on a fixed experimental dataset | Independently increased production answer accuracy |

These examples describe wording boundaries only. Do not copy their tools, responsibilities, or settings into a user's resume unless that user supplied those facts.

### 中文投递语体

使用招聘方读起来直接、自然的陈述。材料中的限制先用于控制措辞，再把本人实际行动写清楚；不要把审校者的旁白逐句搬进投递稿。

| 表达类型 | 合适的写法 | 应避免的写法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 本人负责复现、取证与回归 | 复现缺陷、采集诊断证据并验证修复 | 修复代码由其他人员完成，非本人开发 |
| 已有团队效率结果 | 将所做的具体改动与“团队……”的已知结果连成主句 | 在数字后反复括注“团队整体结果，不全归因于本人” |
| 已明确实验评测 | 在实验环境验证所述指标 | 把实验数据写成线上承诺；或冗长解释每个未负责的环节 |
| 摘要定位 | 仅用已确认的职业方向与核心相关经验概括；不能增加新经历 | 习惯以日志和证据链说明做了什么、怎么验证、结果如何 |
| 稀疏技能材料 | 按材料写“学习过……”并简洁归类 | 据此推定“自学阶段”“经过培训”“基础扎实”或“可独立交付” |
| 技能分组字段 | `title` 写分组名；`subtitle` 无新增信息时留空 | `title` 和 `subtitle` 重复同一分组名或同义说法 |

“团队”“实验环境”和检索指标的真实名称是有意义的事实限定，应在合适位置保留。删去多余的归属旁白，不等于删掉这些限定；也不能改用“独立实现”“主导优化”来回避解释。

## 5. Use a selective summary

For a current student or recent graduate with a clear target, omit the summary by default. The name, target role, and stated availability belong in the header; the degree belongs in education. Repeating this context in a paragraph delays the strongest evidence.

Retain a summary only for a career transition, complex history, or an explicit user request. Use only 1–2 short sentences about positioning and core relevant experience. Include a number only when it is genuinely the strongest positioning evidence; do not stack all downstream counts into an opening ledger. Evidence gathering and diagnostic logs are concrete actions to put under the relevant experience, not "habits" or generic self-praise to add to the summary. Avoid self-evaluative openings such as “习惯……”, “善于……”, and “具有……能力”.

Do not repeat a number in the summary, skills section, and experience merely to make the document feel quantitative. Keep each measurement where its context and attribution are clearest.

## 6. Review against the raw source, then render

Review the drafted resume in a separate pass, comparing it with the original material rather than trusting only the paraphrase:

1. **Truth:** trace every employer, date, quantity, setting label, tool, proficiency claim, and responsibility verb to the source. Check that edited metric names and units still mean the same thing.
2. **Relevance:** verify each experience has a clear purpose for the target and that the most convincing evidence appears first.
3. **Density:** remove duplicated tools, repeated numbers, filler, title/subtitle duplication, and a step-by-step task diary. Keep one main contribution per bullet and preserve distinct relevant evidence.
4. **Boundaries:** keep necessary team, experimental, and measurement qualifications. Express actual responsibilities positively; do not copy explanations about whose code it was or stack parenthetical "not responsible for" notes in the resume. Do not infer a learning channel or provenance label.
5. **Submission readiness:** move suggestions, questions, risk notes, requests for missing data, and review commentary into the chat. Missing optional information should not create empty boilerplate. If identity/contact essentials are missing, label the file as a draft and explain the gap outside the document.

Resolve an unsupported claim by correcting the language, omitting the unverified addition, or asking a narrow question when the missing fact is essential. Do not compensate by inventing another accomplishment.

After review, pass the final text to the fixed renderer. Preserve the selected template and photo preferences. For a template-only or photo-only change, reuse `resume_data_path` through `source_path` so this editorial process does not rewrite unchanged content again.
