## Executive summary

October 2026 held paid spend flat at **USD 2,000** while combined ROAS, CPA, CTR, CPC and conversion rate improved; CPM increased: **ROAS 3.40** (+0.40, +13.33%) and **CPA 14.71 USD** (−1.96, −11.76%), both inside the two explicit targets. The gain is mix, not efficiency — USD 200 moved from Social to Search and none of the six channel-level rates changed. The proposed November 2026 scenario holds Search **1,200 USD** + Social **800 USD** = **2,000 USD** with no transfer, with bounded tests proposed inside the existing channel budgets; nothing has been executed.

## Trend analysis

Two monthly observations only: this is one September→October step, not a trend. There is no daily or weekly detail, no variance or confidence data and no prior-year history, so inferential confidence is limited; the supplied arithmetic itself can be checked exactly; seasonality, long-term direction, statistical significance and any reliable forecast are not assessable.

At flat spend, combined impressions fell to **140,000** (−10,000, −6.67%) while clicks rose to **3,200** (+200, +6.67%), conversions to **136** (+16, +13.33%) and revenue to **6,800.00 USD** (+800.00, +13.33%). Combined CPM rose **0.95 USD (+7.14%)** and combined CPC fell **0.04 USD (−6.25%)**; all changes are computed from unrounded totals, never by subtracting rounded displayed rates.

The mechanism is composition. Every Search base metric rose exactly +20.00% and every Social base metric fell exactly −20.00%, while all six rates in both channels were unchanged (Search: CTR 4.00%, CPC 0.50, CPM 20.00, conversion rate 5.00%, CPA 10.00, ROAS 5.00; Social: 1.00%, 1.00, 10.00, 2.00%, 50.00, 1.00). Search's share of combined impressions moved 33.33% → 42.86% and of combined clicks 66.67% → 75.00%.

The one threshold event: combined ROAS moved from 3.00 — exactly on the ≥3.0 floor with zero buffer — to 3.40. Data-quality flag: rates identical to the cent across both months plus exact ±20.00% volume moves are atypical in live media and consistent with these being synthetic figures.

## What worked

These are metric-level findings, not a channel ranking — only two channels were observed, so no multi-channel winner or loser list is claimed.

- **Combined CPA headroom widened** from 3.33 USD to 5.29 USD against the ≤20 USD ceiling.
- **Combined ROAS gained a 0.40 buffer** where September had none.
- **Combined CTR +0.29 pp (+14.29%)** on 10,000 fewer impressions, with clicks +6.67%.
- **Search absorbed +200 USD** with conversions and revenue both +20.00% and no rate deterioration, suggesting — hypothesis only — that bid and budget headroom existed at 1,200 USD.

All four are consistent with mix arithmetic; none proves a creative, targeting or bidding improvement.

## What needs improvement

- **Social unit economics are unchanged:** ROAS **1.00** and CPA **50.00 USD** — 2.5x the combined ceiling and 5x Search CPA of 10.00 — with revenue of 800.00 USD against 800.00 USD of spend.
- **Social spend fell 20.00%.** Whether tests were run is unknown; investigate its unchanged rates before proposing further changes.
- **Combined CPM +0.95 USD (+7.14%)** is unfavourable but has no target and follows the mix shift toward the higher-CPM channel.
- **The gain is reversible:** at the September 50/50 mix, combined ROAS returns to 3.00 — on the floor with zero buffer.
- **Measurement gaps:** attribution method and window are unknown, unique reach and frequency are unknown (impressions are not unique reach and are not inferred as such), customer quality is unknown (no qualification, LTV or margin data), and no targets were supplied beyond combined CPA and combined ROAS.

## Insights

- Combined improvement is **portfolio arithmetic**: flat spend, a 10.00 pp move toward Search, and unchanged channel rates. Any explanation of why the mix moved is a hypothesis.
- Search at 60.00% of spend is simultaneously the buffer and the concentration risk: a sufficiently large Search ROAS decline could erase the combined ROAS buffer.
- Social spend and revenue were each 1,000.00 USD in September and each 800.00 USD in October, so the issue is its rate structure, not its volume.
- Two periods cannot establish seasonality, a long-term trend, statistical significance or a reliable forecast; everything here is descriptive.

## Recommendations

## Next-period focus

Defend the mix, learn inside each channel, and instrument the gaps. Priority is protecting the 3.40 ROAS buffer and the 5.29 USD CPA headroom at unchanged 2,000 USD total spend, while generating the first in-channel evidence on whether Social's 50.00 USD CPA is structural or movable.

Suggested next steps:

- Carry Search 1,200 USD / Social 800 USD into the November cycle as the proposed scenario and record combined ROAS beside Search spend share, so mix and efficiency stay separable.
- Design the Social diagnostic inside the existing 800 USD and fix an internal Social CPA threshold before any result is read.
- Queue the Search test cell for December with the CPC 0.50 / CPA 10.00 stop rule documented in advance.
- Add attribution model and window, unique reach and frequency, and weekly channel splits to the monthly pull; these remain unknown until actually measured.
