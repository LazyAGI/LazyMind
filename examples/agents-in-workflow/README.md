# Bring AI agents into your working process

[中文](README.CN.md) · [All examples](../README.md)

> Connect sources, tools, and deliverables within one task.

A real task often spans research, organizing ideas, writing a document, making a presentation, and handing over the result. As the toolset grows, so can the work of copying material, explaining context, and checking outputs between tools.

This product introduction presents LazyMind as a way to connect agents and tools around a complex task. It is a starting point for readers who want to understand where LazyMind could fit into their work.

## Look at a task you already do

Choose a recurring task and consider three parts of it:

1. **Where are the inputs?** What sources, requirements, and background does the task need?
2. **How do stages connect?** How does the material from one stage move into writing, analysis, or production?
3. **Where do you review the result?** Which decisions need your judgment, and which deliverables need editing before handoff?

These questions help identify where a workflow is useful: organizing repeated handoffs and giving you opportunities to participate where judgment matters.
