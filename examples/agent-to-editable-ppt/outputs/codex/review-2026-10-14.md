A later [People's Daily reporters' article](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmzk/pc/content/202601/12/content_30134216.html) states more than 1.95 million objects/sets as of the end of 2024. Thus the old number is not simply fabricated, but must not be presented as an undated current total. The safest concise slide heading is “从宫廷旧藏到公共文化宝库”; use the dated later figure only with its citation. No updated verification was found here for the 8000 figure or national ranking, so the proposed copy omits them.
