# Stay involved while Codex creates your presentation

[中文](README.CN.md) · [All examples](../README.md)

> Revise the outline before generation, then select individual elements to adjust in the finished slides.

You often know which parts of a presentation need your judgment: the order of the story, the emphasis on a key point, or a paragraph that feels too long. Leaving every decision until the final file arrives can mean explaining the brief again.

This Palace Museum demo shows how to participate along the way. Start the task in Codex, inspect intermediate content in the LazyMind panel, and direct changes at specific slides and elements.

**[Watch Codex × LazyMind](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1TppR6xEj7/)** · 1:23 · Chinese · Published 2026-10-12

## From a request to a specific edit

- **State the brief.** Describe the topic, style, and stages where you want to review the work.
- **Inspect content before generation.** Review the outline and per-slide content in the panel, and adjust the story before proceeding.
- **Point to the element you want to change.** The later part of the video shows element selection inside a generated slide.
- **Check the page as a whole.** Review how the adjusted content fits the deck's style and message.

## Two moments worth watching

**The per-slide review around 0:52** shows an opportunity to shape the content before the deck is finished. **Element selection around 1:00** shows how a request to change something can refer to a specific part of the page.

The focus here is control during creation. This case is for people who already use Codex and want review and editing to remain part of the presentation workflow. The finished Palace Museum slides provide a concrete visual reference.
