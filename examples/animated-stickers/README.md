# Turn your pet, avatar, or character idea into animated stickers

[中文](README.CN.md) · [All examples](../README.md)

> Give everyday chat reactions a character of your own.

A quick “got it,” a tired reaction, or a celebration can feel more personal when it comes from your pet or a familiar character. Custom stickers give everyday messages a recognizable face.

This demo introduces animated-sticker creation using pets, people, cartoons, or memes, along with the idea of refining a result when it does not match your intention. It is an approachable creative use case with an output that is easy to share.

**[Watch the animated-sticker demo](https://weixin.qq.com/sph/AoG4jZWyfh)** · WeChat Channels · Chinese · Published 2026-09-24

## What could your character express?

Choose an image you have permission to use, then think about situations from your own conversations:

- **Everyday replies:** got it, sounds good, on my way.
- **Work moods:** concentrating, needing a break, finally finished.
- **Reactions with friends:** excitement, surprise, encouragement, celebration.

These are ideas to try. Establish the character and emotion first, then describe the motion and style you want.

## Start your first set

Prepare a clear reference image, identify the character features you want to retain, and choose a few reactions you use often. If the result differs from your intention, give specific feedback about the expression, motion, or style.

This is a useful starting point for personal chats, community interactions, and character exploration. Begin with a small set of familiar reactions, see how recognizable and useful they feel in conversation, and expand from there.
