# Make an AI presentation fit the story you want to tell

[中文](README.CN.md) · [All examples](../README.md)

> Bring decisions about wording and layout into the creation process.

“The content is there, but I still would not present it.” A generated deck can feel too dense, lack emphasis, or use a style that does not fit the occasion. Reworking everything after delivery can turn presentation preparation into repeated cleanup.

This video focuses on AI presentation layouts and wording, introducing ways to improve stiff slides and reduce repeated revisions. It is aimed at people who have tried AI-generated decks and want more control over the result.

**[Watch the original presentation-refinement video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1cjHS6wEad/)** · Chinese · Published 2026-10-08

## Watch with three questions in mind

- **What is the point of each slide?** Decide what the audience should remember before judging whether content needs trimming or restructuring.
- **Does the style fit the occasion?** A product proposal, knowledge-sharing session, and formal report may call for different visual choices.
- **Can the revision request be more specific?** Replace “make it better” with a clear request about the title, text density, image placement, or a particular element.

## Apply the idea to your own deck

Start with an outline and describe the audience, purpose, and preferred style. Use LazyMind's content review and slide-editing capabilities to refine it. Picking one slide and explaining its problem gives you a concrete starting point.
